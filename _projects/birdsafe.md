Role:  UX Researcher and Designer  
Tools: Figma  
Timeframe: 3/2024 - 6/2024  


BirdSafe aimed to encourage more conscious window purchasing to prevent bird collision related deaths and injuries.  

As a UX Researcher and Designer, I worked collaboratively with my team to identify stakeholders, conceptualize interventions, design a prototype, designed a User Interview protocol, and updated our prototype according to user feedback. Our results were compiled in a 15 page report.   

**Stakeholder Research**  
Stakeholders and intervention points were found by interviewing Architecture students, students who frequently study in buildings with large windows, and by consulting reports on the frequency of bird collisions. Research was also conducted on exisiting solutions for this issue, such as window stickers, patterned windows, and opaque windows.   

After conducting research, we decided our best point of intervention was with the public: spreading awareness about the issue.   

**Conceptualization**  
We conceptualized three possible interventions:  
•	An Educational Website  
•	A Retail Website   
•	A Game where you play in the perspective of a bird and try to identify if a window is present  

**Prototyping**  
We settled on a hybrid of all three concepts: A Educational Website that also includes options for bird-safe window retailers, as well as a minigame where the user is asked if an image contains a window or not.  

**User Research**  
I designed two study procedures: An Observational Study and a Thinkaloud study in order to evaluate the success of our intervention. We aimed to see if the website served two functions: if it educated the user on the issue of bird collisions and if it encourage them to try using the bird safe window precautions.   

Our research found that while the website was effective at raising awareness about the impact windows had on birds, and our participants retained knowledge with a high success rate.
