Role: UX Researcher  
Tools: Figma, Miro  
Time Frame: 1/2026 - Present  

SkyHub is a automated vehicle inspection dashboard designed for used car dealerships. It was designed in collaboration with Amazon Autos for a Capstone project. As the UX researcher, I was responsible for creating user interview procedures, conducting market analysis and stakeholder research, as well as designing and creating wireframes. 

**Problem Context**  
Amazon Autos is an online marketplace that allows dealerships, partnered with Amazon, to list new and used vehicles. However, it relies solely on the information dealers send to Amazon Autos. This leaves some inconsistencies in image quality and documentation, especially for mid-sized car dealerships with more limited technical capabilities. This, in turn, makes it hard for consumers, who heavily rely on listing images when shopping for used cars. 

**User Interviews**  
We conducted user interviews with Amazon Autos project managers, mid-sized car dealerships local to the Greater Seattle Area, and people who had recently bought a used car online. From our interviews, we found the following:  
- Customers biggest concern with used cars was how damaged they were, and they wanted to see the damage clearly 
- Mid-sized local dealerships had technical limitations for taking listing pictures, such as only using cell phone pictures. Manual inspections were time consuming. 

We chose to solve these issues by creating an automated vehicle inspection VLM that automatcally highlighted damages in car listing photos, making the creation of these listings much more efficient. This would be entirely dealer-facing, and the inspections would be automatically sent to Amazon Autos after approval to streamline the data transfer process.  

Our market analysis of similar damage visualization tools found that some of these tools used dot markers that obscured the damage, which we wanted to improve upon. 

**Prototyping** 
Our initial prototype focused on two core features: 
- A dashboard to organize all of the dealership's vehicle inspections.
- AI assissted ehicle inspection.
    - Image upload that supports up to 35 high rsolution images
    - AI detection with a Intern VL 3.5 Model
    - Damage visualization with boundary boxes, color-coded by severity
