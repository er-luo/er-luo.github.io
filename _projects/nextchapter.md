Role: Front End Developer  
Tools: CSS, HTML, React.js, Figma  
Time Frame: 1/2024 - 3/2024  
Website: https://project-nextchapter-f60bb.web.app/  

NextChapter is a platform designed to combat overconsumption and builds community by promoting book swapping with the community.  

As a Front End Developer, I was responsible for ensuring that our application was both responsive and accessible in design. This was done through LightHouse Accessibility testing and Media Queries. I worked with the UX Designer to adapt portions of our initial prototype to be more accessible, balancing accessibility with the original more pastel aesthetic of the original design.
